What are your OPD timings?

Our OPD is open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Emergency support is available 24/7.

Do I need prior appointment?

Walk-ins are accepted, but booking in advance helps reduce waiting time and ensures doctor availability.

Which payment methods are accepted?

We accept cash, UPI, credit and debit cards, and most major insurance providers where applicable.

Can I consult online?

Yes, tele-consultation is available for selected departments. Use the appointment form to request an online slot.
