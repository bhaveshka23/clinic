Welcome To Tolani Clinic

Dr. Gulshan Bheem Tolani

Dr. Gulshan Bheem Tolani is a verified General Physician, Internal Medicine specialist, and Diabetologist in Nashik with over 11 years of experience. He completed his MBBS from Pravara Institute of Medical Sciences (2015) and MD in General Medicine from Rural Medical College, Loni (2019) He treats a wide range of conditions including fever, cough, asthma, COPD, diabetes, hypertension, thyroid, kidney, and stomach disorders, delivering compassionate and advanced medical care in a comfortable environment. He currently practices at Tolani Clinic (Ganjmal), Vijan Hospital & Research Centre (Canada Corner), and Dr. Tolani Clinic (Baghwan Pura), making him a trusted choice for healthcare in Nashik.
