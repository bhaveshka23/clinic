Corona Infection Clinical Support

Timely consultation, monitoring, and recovery guidance for viral infection phases.

Clinical Overview

Our corona infection care is focused on early triage, home-management safety, and escalation criteria where required. We monitor oxygen-related symptoms, inflammatory warning signs, and progression risk factors.

Post-viral follow-up supports respiratory recovery, fatigue management, and return-to-routine planning.

Care Pathways

Early Triage and Severity Check

Risk categorization based on symptoms, age, and comorbidity profile.

Guided Recovery Pathway

Evidence-based medication, hydration, and isolation guidance.

Post-Viral Monitoring

Recovery review for persistent cough, weakness, and breathing concerns.

Key Patient Benefits

  • Safer home recovery decisions
  • Clear escalation warning signs
  • Reduced anxiety with guided care
  • Structured post-infection follow-up
