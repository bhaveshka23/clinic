Clinical Overview

This combined care model addresses two high-impact chronic conditions together. We track glycemic control, blood pressure variability, kidney profile, and cardiovascular risk to reduce future complications.

Treatment includes individualized medication titration, nutrition planning, exercise guidance, and milestone-based follow-up.

Care Pathways

Metabolic Baseline Setup

Baseline glucose, BP, and renal profile established before treatment mapping.

Personalized Drug Titration

Dose optimization with regular clinical review and safety checks.

Complication Prevention

Continuous surveillance for eye, kidney, and heart risk indicators.

Key Patient Benefits

  • Improved sugar and BP control
  • Reduced risk of organ damage
  • Better treatment consistency
  • Actionable monthly health targets
