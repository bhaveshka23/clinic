Liver Disease Assessment and Management

Targeted care for fatty liver, liver inflammation, and chronic hepatic risk.

Clinical Overview

Liver health management combines biochemical monitoring with lifestyle and metabolic correction. We assess liver enzymes, imaging reports, alcohol risk, weight profile, and related metabolic conditions.

Treatment pathways are tailored to disease stage with regular evaluation to reduce progression toward advanced liver damage.

Care Pathways

Hepatic Risk Evaluation

Comprehensive review of LFT trends and metabolic contributors.

Lifestyle and Medical Control

Nutrition, weight, and medicine strategy to improve liver profile.

Stage-Based Follow-up

Periodic monitoring to evaluate treatment response and progression.

Key Patient Benefits

  • Better enzyme trend control
  • Reduced fatty liver progression risk
  • Personalized long-term plan
  • Integrated metabolic correction
